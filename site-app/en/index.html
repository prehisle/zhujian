How it works

From noted to done, in three steps

1

Note it

The global hotkey Ctrl+Alt+N brings up a slip. Press Enter and it's saved to Notes; the window goes away on its own. Screenshots paste straight in.

Notes · Today
Pick up the health report tomorrowjust now
Tidy the second bookshelf this weekend17:00
Call Mum, ask how the check-up went16:42
↵ Save Esc Hide
2

Sort it

In Notes, tag it or turn it into a task. Every edit keeps the previous version, so old drafts can be recovered; deleting puts it in Trash first, and you can restore it any time.

3

Get it done

To do, In progress, To confirm, Done. Drag tasks along; finished ones go to Archive, where you can look them up but not delete them.

Small things

Small things you use every day

Every version is kept

Each edit keeps the version before it. If you break something or cut too much, the old draft is still there.

Deleting goes to Trash first

Restore any time. Deleting for good only happens inside Trash.

Archive is a record

Finished tasks go to Archive, where you can look them up but not delete them. Look back and see what you got done.

Your own board columns

Add, rename or remove them on the desktop. As many columns as your process has steps.

Keep work and life apart

Each space is its own notebook, kept apart. Type /space in the slip to switch.

Hands on the keyboard

Every action on a card has a single key; ] and [ move it one column forward or back.

EEdit CCopy LTags SDue PPriority DDelete []Move

Take it with you

On desktop, export a whole space to Markdown in one go, images and comments included; any editor can open it.

Yours

What you write stays with you

Data on your own devices

It lives in a SQLite database on your own device and works offline. Sync is optional: text and images are encrypted on your device before they leave, so the server only ever handles ciphertext.

No analytics

With sync off, builds downloaded from zhujian.app only check it for updates; store builds contact nothing at all.

No AI touching your words

No polishing, no summaries, no auto-sorting. What you write is what stays.
